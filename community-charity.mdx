## Meaningful charity ideas

- Sponsor iftar meals locally.
- Support trusted relief organizations.
- Prepare food for neighbors or students away from family.
- Share skills and time, not only money.

## Build community spirit

- Join masjid programs and volunteer teams.
- Create a family sadaqah jar for children.
- Reach out to someone fasting alone.
