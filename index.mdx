## Ramadan goals you can start today

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set one intention">
    Choose one personal intention for this Ramadan and review it every morning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a realistic routine">
    Start small: 10 minutes of Qur'an, sincere dua, and one act of kindness daily.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay connected">
    Pray with community when possible, check on family, and support those in need.
  </Step>
</Steps>
