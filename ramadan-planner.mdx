## Sample day structure

| Time | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Before Fajr | Suhoor, hydration, intention |
| After Fajr | Qur'an recitation + adhkar |
| Midday | Work/study with mindful breaks |
| Before Maghrib | Dua and preparation for iftar |
| After Isha | Taraweeh and reflection |

## Keep it sustainable

- Prioritize quality over quantity.
- Protect sleep where possible.
- Plan a weekly reset to adjust goals.
