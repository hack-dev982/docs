## Suhoor ideas

- Oats with yogurt, dates, nuts, and fruit
- Eggs with whole-grain toast and avocado
- Lentil soup and a banana smoothie

## Iftar ideas

- Dates + water, then soup and salad
- Grilled chicken/fish with rice and vegetables
- Chickpea bowls with olive oil and lemon

## Hydration tips

- Drink water steadily between iftar and suhoor.
- Include hydrating foods (cucumber, watermelon, soups).
- Reduce very salty and heavily fried foods.
