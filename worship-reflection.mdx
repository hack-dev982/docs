## Core worship checklist

- Five daily prayers on time
- Daily Qur'an recitation (even a small amount)
- Morning and evening adhkar
- Daily dua for yourself and others

## Reflection prompts

1. What habit is bringing me closer to Allah this week?
2. Which distraction should I reduce?
3. Who can I support through prayer or action today?
